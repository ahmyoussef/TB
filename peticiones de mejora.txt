| OCR Input | Detected Hours | Validation Action | Corrected Output |

| `Time left: 87 h 30 m` | 87 | **AUTO-CORRECT** (Remove "8") | `7 h 30 m` |
| `Time left: 80 h 15 m` | 80 | **AUTO-CORRECT** | `0 h 15 m` |
| `Time left: 89 h 45 m` | 89 | **AUTO-CORRECT** | `9 h 45 m` |
| `Time left: 25 h 10 m` | 25 | **ERROR** (Exceeds max) | Prompt user |
| `Time left: 19 h 59 m` | 19 | Valid (maximum) | `19 h 59 m` |
